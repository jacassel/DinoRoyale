# Dino Royale 0.6 — launch roster preparation

Albertosaurus completes the seven-species playable and AI roster. It is an original,
long-legged theropod with a distinct silhouette, terracotta/charcoal skin, a rig,
16 animation clips, three quick bites and a committed pursuing heavy bite.

Compared with Rex, final health is 90%, quick damage 87%, fully charged damage
86%, running speed 114% and sprint speed 120%. Faster acceleration, turning and
lower sprint drain support pursuit. A longer third-hit recovery and costly heavy
preserve a meaningful tradeoff against Rex's health, reach and raw damage.

Brachiosaurus retains its stats and artwork, with clearer forefoot preparation,
neck lean, weight transfer and follow-through. Anky's three quick attacks and heavy
use broad, braced tail sweeps with collision following the actual club. Anky club
segmentation and Pachy dome ridges now use their baked color/normal detail instead
of the flat generic horn material that obscured it. Original older sources remain.

F5 opens shared match setup, locally or in the multiplayer host lobby:

- Team score: **5 Short / 10 Standard (default) / 15 Extended**. FFA remains 5.
- Enable/ban any of the seven species; at least one must remain. Selection, bots,
  pack followers and respawns obey the host's allowed roster.
- Independent additional **Team A / Team B bots**, including zero and intentionally
  unequal teams. Each team needs a participant; the total capacity stays ten.
- Human, bot and team totals remain visible. Over-capacity requests are preserved
  with a warning and block starting. Guests cannot override host rules.

Offline play now shares the established multiplayer leader/follower lifecycle:
ten main slots at most, with two followers per Raptor/Pachy leader outside those
slots. Followers retain leader kill credit and do not become scoring targets or
extra result rows. Local setting changes begin a fresh valid round on resume.

Existing stamina, hunger, eating, plants, carcasses, injury, swimming, optional
blood, map visibility, K/D/A and ten-second respawn systems remain in use.

Use the complete new Windows folder on every PC. Compatibility is **2026100706**.
The EOS provider and identity remain unchanged. Keep the previous working package.
Configured online values and Saved caches are excluded from public archives.

See VERSION06_TEST_REPORT.md and VERSION06_BALANCE_REPORT.md for exact accepted
runs, failures/retests and limits. This is a Windows Development alpha, not a
Steam-integrated production release. Human competitive balance, animation feel,
and a fresh two-PC WAN session remain external validation work. Albertosaurus
currently uses a pitched version of the existing Rex sound bank.
