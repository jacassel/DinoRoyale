# Dinosaur Battle architecture and design

## Gameplay ownership

`ADinosaurCharacter` owns health, combat, animation and food components. Human input calls shared gameplay methods; `ADinosaurAIController` invokes the same combat/food methods. CharacterMovement provides swept capsule movement, acceleration, ground contact and jumping. There is no root-motion authority or network layer.

`FSpeciesData` reads one centralized record per species from Config/DefaultGame.ini. Attack damage, timing, charge multiplier, movement/turn/acceleration, capsule, camera, regeneration and defense are configurable. Session settings include respawn delay, mouse sensitivity and eating rate.

`UHealthComponent` applies damage and delayed regeneration. Movement and attack rate derive from current health each update, preventing permanent injury modifiers after recovery. Below 25 percent health, charging is rejected and any existing charge is cancelled.

`UCombatComponent` owns charge, brace and attack recovery state. A timed sphere sweep covers the species' forward melee area; each actor is considered once per attack, with a wall trace and facing check. Shielding cancels pending attack damage and horizontal movement. Frontal damage is reduced according to species data.

`UDinoAnimationComponent` selects an explicit state: death, brace, charge, eating, heavy/quick attack, jump, locomotion or idle. Gameplay recovery never waits for an animation notify. All imported clips use the original Blender skeleton; animation is in-place and movement remains capsule-authoritative.

## Species

T-Rex is a large solitary predator with the highest single-hit damage. Raptor moves and turns fastest, has low health, and can leap with its charged attack. Allied raptors follow a human leader when available, otherwise their lowest-ID AI raptor; the leader role persists through death and respawn. They spread into formation and offset pursuit positions. Triceratops is slower, has the largest health pool and strongest frontal brace, and uses its head/horns in combat. Prey is weak, flees predators and leaves an edible carcass.

GameMode owns match rules, team membership, damage-contribution attribution and K/D/A. FFA ends at five scoring kills; 5v5 ends at ten team kills. Raptor followers and prey are excluded as scoring victims. A configurable shared pack score credits follower kills to their leader. Assists use a configurable twelve-second recent-damage window. Major respawns take ten seconds.

`UDinoMovementComponent` implements surface swimming with collision sweeps, horizontal acceleration, damping and a bounded vertical correction. Analytic water queries share pond/creek geometry with terrain generation. Shallow water retains walking; sufficient depth activates swimming with entry/exit hysteresis, a paddle animation and health-based speed modifiers. Both AI and human controls use the same movement component.

Health totals 1100 / 520 / 1900 and quick damage 180 / 100 / 145 are first-pass species tuning, distinct from the relative 0–100 design scores.

## World and AI

`ALostValleyWorld` produces deterministic, continuous rolling terrain, a shallow creek, instanced original trees/rocks/ferns/grass, feeding locations and perimeter barriers. Broad routes and spawn clearings leave room for the largest capsule. Visible obstacles and navigation obstacles come from the same generated placement data.

Navigation uses a 193 by 193 clearance-aware A* grid (600 cm cells) with diagonal corner checks, line-of-sight path smoothing and local repulsion. This is the prototype's NavMesh equivalent. A shared conservative clearance supports every species. AI thinks about five times per second while steering every frame. Progress monitoring triggers replanning or a nudge/jump when stuck.

Food is represented by regrowing plant actors and dead dinosaur nutrition. Holding E consumes a suitable nearby source and heals at 12 percent maximum health per second. Moving, attacking, bracing or taking damage interrupts eating.

## Assets and performance

All dinosaurs, skeletal rigs, clips and environment meshes originate in local Blender scripts. Original surface texture tiles are generated by a retained local script. No marketplace art is used. Instanced environment geometry, foliage culling, limited shadow distance and a 60 FPS cap keep the target conservative for the RTX 3060.

Source control is local Git. Build outputs, caches, logs and the portable compiler are ignored. Native Blender sources and imported Unreal assets are retained.

## Combat polish architecture

`UStaminaComponent` owns resource spending, regeneration and exhaustion hysteresis. Character input and AI request abilities through the same character/combat APIs. AI stuck recovery now calls the stamina-aware jump API. All species resource and commitment tuning lives in `FSpeciesData` and editable INI sections.

Quick strikes use a three-hit sequence, one buffered input during the final 180ms, a 12% third-strike bonus and extra third recovery. A one-second gap after recovery resets the sequence. Bracing cannot cancel an attack; pausing cancels charge/input but preserves cooldown. Exhausted quick attacks cost no additional stamina, deal 65% damage and take 30% longer, allowing resource regeneration and preventing a permanent lock.

Heavies commit a forward direction, active drive, reduced steering and a short swept hit window. A weak-pointer hit set ensures only one hit per target per attack. Unbraced hits knock back and interrupt charges; misses add recovery. Bracing has continuous drain plus an impact cost, with lower Triceratops costs.

AI profiles are assigned per combatant with a seeded +/-15% temperament variation. Aggressive weights quick pressure, defensive weights guards/counters, skirmisher reacts with lateral spacing, and balanced favors varied heavy openings. All react to stamina, injury, support and vulnerability using the existing controller and navigation system.
