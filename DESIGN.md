# Dinosaur Battle design

Local single-player game: one human and nine major AI dinosaurs. Species tuning is centralized.
Shared character abilities are independent of player input; AI uses those same abilities.
Health, combat, species data, navigation, ecology, camera, and UI are separated for iteration.
Original Blender sources and reproducible generation scripts are retained in the repository.

Priority: reliable controls, combat and navigation before cosmetic detail.
